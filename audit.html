Away SEO audit robot

If you found AwaySEO-Audit in your server logs, this page explains who runs it and what it does.

Who runs it

AwaySEO-Audit is operated by Ibrahima C., independent SEO consultant, under the name Away SEO. It reads public web pages to prepare SEO audits, either requested by the site owner or, in a limited form, as a short sample.

How it behaves

  • It identifies itself in its User-Agent (AwaySEO-Audit, with this page’s address).
  • It follows your robots.txt, including the paths you forbid.
  • It reads public pages only: it never logs in and never submits a form.
  • It waits between requests, slows down when your server answers 429 or 503, and stops when it is refused.
  • Sample mode (no agreement from the site owner): 30 pages at most, one request at a time, about 1.5 seconds apart.

How to stop it

Add these two lines to your robots.txt: User-agent: AwaySEO-Audit then Disallow: /. The robot applies them at its next visit. You can also write to me through the contact form and I will remove your site from my lists.
