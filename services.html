Technical SEO Audit

A deep-dive into the technical health of your site: how search engines crawl, render, and index your pages. This audit examines the infrastructure that search engines use to discover and understand your content. Without a solid technical foundation, even the best content may struggle to rank. The audit covers server response times, redirect chains, canonical configuration, XML sitemaps, robots.txt directives, JavaScript rendering issues, and mobile usability. You receive a clear, prioritized list of fixes ranked by impact and effort.

  • Crawlability & indexability analysis
  • Page speed & Core Web Vitals
  • Site architecture & URL structure
  • Robots.txt, sitemaps, canonical tags
  • Mobile-friendliness assessment
$49

On-Page SEO

Making sure every page is properly understood by Google — from titles to content structure. On-page SEO is about aligning your content with what users search for and how search engines interpret it. This audit reviews your title tags, meta descriptions, heading hierarchy (H1 through H6), keyword targeting, content depth, internal linking patterns, and structured data markup. The goal is to ensure each page sends clear, consistent signals about its topic and intent, so Google can rank it for the right queries. The report includes specific recommendations for each page type on your site.

  • Title tags & meta descriptions
  • Heading structure (H1–H6)
  • Content quality & keyword targeting
  • Internal linking strategy
  • Schema markup / structured data
$79

Off-Page SEO & Link Building

Building authority through a clean, sustainable backlink profile. Off-page SEO is about the signals that exist outside your own website — primarily backlinks from other sites. This audit maps your current link profile, identifies toxic or spammy links that could harm your rankings, analyzes your competitors' link strategies, and recommends a realistic acquisition plan. The focus is on quality over quantity: a few authoritative, relevant links will always outperform dozens of low-value ones. You receive a disavow file for toxic links and a prioritized outreach plan.

  • Backlink profile audit
  • Competitor link analysis
  • Toxic link identification & disavowal
  • Outreach & acquisition strategy
$79

Local SEO & Google Business Profile

Maximizing your visibility in local searches and Google Maps. For businesses serving a specific geographic area, local SEO is often the highest-ROI channel. This audit reviews your Google Business Profile completeness, review velocity and sentiment, local keyword rankings, Map Pack positioning, and citation consistency across directories (NAP — Name, Address, Phone). The report identifies quick wins to improve your local pack visibility and a longer-term strategy to dominate your service area.

  • Google Business Profile optimization
  • Review management strategy
  • Map Pack visibility tactics
  • Local citation consistency (NAP)
$59

AEO & GEO — AI Optimization

Getting cited in direct answers and AI-generated responses from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. As search evolves from blue links to AI-generated answers, being visible in these new formats is increasingly important. This diagnostic assesses how well your site is positioned to be cited by answer engines and generative AI tools. It reviews your content structure, factual clarity, structured data, entity associations, and brand mention patterns across AI platforms. The report provides a roadmap to increase your chances of being the source AI tools cite when answering queries in your niche.

  • Answer engine visibility assessment
  • Content optimization for AI citations
  • Structured data for answer engines
  • Brand mention tracking across AI tools
$59
